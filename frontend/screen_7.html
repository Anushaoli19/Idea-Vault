Automated Hostel Mess Food Waste Auditor with Computer Vision

01

The Problem

In the NIE South Campus dining halls, an estimated 120 kg of prepared food is discarded daily. Kitchen management currently relies on static spreadsheet logs recorded at the end of each week, offering zero actionable correlation between day-to-day menu options and actual plate remnants.

Without a rapid, quantifiable feedback loop, kitchen procurement teams repeatedly over-purchase perishable staples like sambar vegetables, rice, and fresh dairy. This inefficiency drives up mess operational expenses while escalating our institution's biological waste output.

02

Proposed Solution & Hardware Architecture

We propose an automated audit station seamlessly retrofitted above the mess tray collection belt. The architecture decouples physical weight acquisition from computational image classification:

System Pipeline Layout
photo_camera Overhead Vision Hub Raspberry Pi 4 with Camera Module 3 captures tray discard profile.
memory YOLOv8 Edge Inference Quantized Nano model classifies leftover types (rice, sambar, chapati, curry).
scale ESP32 Load Cells Dual HX711 transducers record instantaneous plate delta-mass.

By linking specific meal items with gram-accurate waste increments, the local edge service uploads structured time-series records directly to an institutional analytics portal via campus Wi-Fi.
