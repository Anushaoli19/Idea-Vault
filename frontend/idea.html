Automated Hostel Mess Food Waste Auditor with Computer Vision

Ananya Deshmukh check_circle
Mechanical Engineering, 6th Sem • NIE South Campus
schedule Posted Feb 12, 2025 4 team slots open
Estimated Campus Impact ~120 kg/day Hostel Block 1 & 2
Model Inference Target < 140ms Edge device processing
Projected Cost Savings ₹ 42,000 /mo Procurement optimization
01

The Problem

In the NIE South Campus dining halls, an estimated 120 kg of prepared food is discarded daily. Kitchen management currently relies on static spreadsheet logs recorded at the end of each week, offering zero actionable correlation between day-to-day menu options and actual plate remnants.

02

Proposed Architecture & System Design

Dual stereoscopic RGB cameras positioned over tray conveyor belts stream 1080p frames to an edge NVIDIA Jetson / Raspberry Pi 4 compute node. Frames are processed using a fine-tuned MobileNetV3 model to segment plate remnants into 4 classes (rice, bread, curry, dry waste).

03

Hardware / BOM & Edge Specs

Raspberry Pi 4 (8GB), Raspberry Pi High Quality Camera Module (12.3MP), HX711 Load Cell Tare Amplifier, 50kg Strain Gauge Load Sensors, 10.1 inch Ruggedized Capacitive Touch Display.
