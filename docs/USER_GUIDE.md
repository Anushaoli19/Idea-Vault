# User Guide: NIE Idea Vault

Welcome to NIE Idea Vault! This guide walks you through browsing projects, submitting innovative ideas, detecting duplicates, and collaborating with peers.

---

## 1. Exploring Ideas (`index.html`)
- **Browsing**: The main dashboard displays project cards organized by department and category.
- **Filtering by Category**: Click any filter chip (`All`, `AI/ML`, `Web`, `IoT`, `App`, `Other`) to narrow down projects.
- **Searching**: Use the search input (or press `Cmd+K` / `Ctrl+K`) to search titles, keywords, or student names in real-time.
- **Sorting**: Toggle between `Most Upvoted` and `Newest` using the top-right segmented pill.
- **Interactions**:
  - Click the **arrow_upward** button on any card to upvote. Votes update instantly and prevent double-voting.
  - Click the **handshake (Collab)** button to express interest in joining the team.
  - Click any card title to open its full deep-dive page (`idea.html?id=...`).

---

## 2. Submitting an Idea (`post.html`)
1. Click **Post Idea** in the top navigation header.
2. Fill in the project details:
   - **Project Title**: E.g. "Smart Campus EV Shuttle Tracker".
   - **Department**: Select your department (CSE, ECE, ISE, AIML, Mech, Civil).
   - **Category**: Select the primary domain (`AI/ML`, `Web`, `IoT`, `App`, `Other`).
   - **Project Phase**: Concept, Prototype, or Validation.
   - **Project Pitch / Description**: Core problem and proposed solution.
   - **Tech Stack**: Comma-separated tags (e.g. `Python, OpenCV, ESP32`).
3. Click **Check & Submit Proposal**.
4. **Duplicate Warning**:
   - If a similar proposal already exists (e.g. searching or submitting something similar to "Automated Hostel Mess Food Waste Auditor"), the **Duplicate Warning Banner** will appear!
   - You can review the match percentage (e.g. 88%), view the existing idea, upvote/join that team instead, or click **Proceed with my separate submission** to post anyway.

---

## 3. Idea Deep-Dive (`idea.html`)
- View full problem background, proposed architecture, hardware specs, and open collaboration roles.
- Review list of other interested students and click **Connect with Project Lead** to open an email draft.
- Upvote or bookmark the project directly from the action bar.
